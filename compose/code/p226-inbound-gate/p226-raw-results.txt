=====QUERY: AI education LATAM 2026 adoption regulation players
A UNESCO IESALC study of 200 institutions in 19 countries found 87% were using AI in at least one area, while 74% reported using it in teaching and learning.
UNESCO notes the region's teachers adopted AI quickly; 56% of secondary school teachers in Brazil had used AI over the previous year, compared to an OECD average of 36%. Uruguay's figures are higher still, at 75% of public-school teachers, per Ceibal.
