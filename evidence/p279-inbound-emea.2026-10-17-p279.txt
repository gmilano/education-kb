A Cloud Security Alliance research note states: the Digital Omnibus proposal requires approval from the European Parliament and Council, and until a formal legislative extension is enacted, the legal obligation remains August 2, 2026.
It also notes the EU AI Act, taking full effect in August 2026, classifies education AI as high-risk.
One source says Annex III, point 3 obligations were originally due 2 August 2026 but have been delayed to 2 December 2027 under the Digital Omnibus, approved by the European Parliament on 16 June 2026.
Another lists 2 August 2026 as the general date of application of the Regulation, including Article 50 transparency obligations, and places the Annex III education obligations on 2 December 2027.
Under Article 26, schools using a purchased tool must ensure the system complies with the AI Act and inform students, parents and teachers about the use of AI.
Most state schools qualify as bodies governed by public law and must complete an Article 27 Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment before deploying any Annex III point 3 high-risk tool.
