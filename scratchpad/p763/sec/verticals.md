### 🟢 The platforms a studio should actually compose on, and why they are not in the table above

They are not platforms. The register's permissive estate is **standards, schemas, pipelines and conformance suites** — `CEDS-Data-Warehouse` (Apache-2.0), `CEDS-IDS` (Apache-2.0), the Ed-Fi technology suite (Apache-2.0 across `9` repositories read), `lrsql` (Apache-2.0), `splink` (MIT), `apache/superset` (Apache-2.0, `11,585 B` · `ff254eb676d6`), `duckdb/duckdb` (MIT, `1,072 B` · `075c33400ffc`) and `dagster-io/dagster` (Apache-2.0, `11,348 B` · `e25b0c5be442`). 🔵 **That is the shape of the opportunity: there is no open-source education data-warehouse *product*, only an excellent permissive kit for building one** — which is the engagement, and it is named concretely in `compose/patterns.md` as `P311`–`P317`.
