**`6` platform rows, every grant byte-read `2026-11-11`** with length and sha256₁₂. 🔵 **Two of the six are filed as warnings rather than as starting points**, and both warnings are about the licence, not the code.

### 🔴 Say this in the first meeting

🟢 **The institution almost certainly already owns the capture estate.** Opencast and BigBlueButton are the open systems of record for lecture video and live classes, and this base has held both for hundreds of passes. **The engagement is not a platform replacement — it is an accessibility pipeline bolted onto a platform the client already runs**, which is a far shorter sale and a far shorter delivery.

🔴 **Then say the licence sentence, because it is the one that decides the architecture.** The transcription tool a European university most likely found by itself — `aTrain`, from a public university, GDPR-local by design — is **`AGPL-3.0`**. So is `aeneas`, the only mature forced aligner. In a hosted pipeline both reach the client's own distribution obligations. 🟢 **Every function they perform is available permissively** from the rows on [`repos/foundations.md`](../repos/foundations.md); the AGPL route is a convenience, and it is the expensive kind.
